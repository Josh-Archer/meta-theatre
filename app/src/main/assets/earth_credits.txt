The provider describes these Earth maps as adaptations of NASA imagery and geographic data.
Downloaded 2026-10-04. These are artistic composites, not live Earth or city-light observations.

Changes by Meta Theatre: downsampling; linear-space lighting and twilight masks;
separation and intensity adjustment of city lights; terrain-normal shading;
ocean highlights; cloud shadows; transparent cloud deck; atmospheric rim;
UV-mapped sphere meshes and Blender, glTF and Unreal material authoring.
The detailed orbital renderer retains native 8K day/city-light/cloud detail,
4K ocean maps, directional cloud relief, land/cloud shadows and ocean Fresnel.

Attribution must travel with redistributed source textures and derived Earth assets.
