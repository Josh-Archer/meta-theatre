Meta Theatre solar environment

Planet, Sun and Saturn ring maps: Solar System Scope / INOVE.
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: resampling to 2048 x 1024; JPEG encoding; ring alpha reduced to a
2048 x 1 radial profile. Lighting, scale, geometry, and arrangement are authored
for this app. The source maps include artistic color treatment and filled gaps.

Moon: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit, Ernie Wright.
LROC Wide Angle Camera color mosaic: NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University.
LOLA elevation: NASA/GSFC/MIT.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
Uses the December 2025 LROC color map and LOLA float elevation in kilometers
relative to a 1737.4 km sphere. Changes: color resampling to 2048 x 1024,
JPEG 98 4:4:4, and physical-slope OpenGL tangent normals at 2048 x 1024.
No geometric displacement or exaggerated elevation. These NASA color maps
include aesthetic exposure, white-balance and polar gap filling by their authors.

Stars: Deep Star Maps 2020, Ernie Wright.
NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/
Uses the separate 8192 x 4096 celestial-coordinate Milky Way and Hipparcos/Tycho
bright-star layers without constellation illustrations. Original HDR sources
are preserved. Changes: independent diffuse/bright-star exposures, luminance
toe and a hue-preserving peak-channel highlight shoulder, sRGB encoding,
JPEG quality 98 with 4:4:4 sampling.
The artistic exposure retains the catalog structure and stellar colors;
this is not a calibrated naked-eye or camera exposure simulation.

Surface lighting uses artist-tuned Lunar-Lambert and Minnaert profiles,
with bounded grazing-angle response and a small cloud-haze contribution.
Photometric model reference: USGS ISIS photemplate documentation.
https://isis.astrogeology.usgs.gov/8.1.0/Application/presentation/PrinterFriendly/photemplate/photemplate.html
The coefficients are presentation choices, not fitted planetary measurements.

Planet mean radii: NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics.
https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/phys_par.html
Moon radius 1737.4 km, Sun radius 695700 km: NASA fact sheets.
https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html
https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/sunfact.html
Saturn main C-through-A rings, 74500 to 136780 km from center:
https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/satringfact.html

All bodies share one mean-radius scale. Spacing is compressed and arranged for
the theatre; positions are not current astronomical ephemerides. The moving Sun
follows the headset's local civil-time cycle. Earth credits are in earth_credits.txt.
