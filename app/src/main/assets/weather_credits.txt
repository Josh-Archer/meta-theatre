Meta Theatre transforms current model cloud coverage and weather codes into
modeled orbital cloud density. This is an artistic interpretation of sampled
weather conditions, not satellite imagery or a weather warning service.
The original offline Earth maps retain their attribution in earth_credits.txt.

Cloud density library and editable models authored for Meta Theatre in Blender;
shared-atlas preview materials authored in the separate Unreal asset sandbox.
