unlock, an actual stereo display capture showed the ISS flyby rendering.
Close material inspection, selection/return behavior and a controlled timing

## Geometry and materials

- The runtime and staged GLBs have 37,865 triangles, four material primitives,
  eight images, unchanged double-sided flags and the original 109 m span.
- Every indexed position and vertex normal matches the baseline exactly.
  Photovoltaic UVs and the embedded original 1024² color PNG also match exactly.
- Normal/tangent vectors are finite and unit length. Tangents are orthogonal
  to normals and have valid handedness. MikkTSpace required 134 fallback corner
  tangents on degenerate source geometry before lossless vertex welding.
- Khronos validation: zero errors, zero warnings. One informational entry
  reports 53 index-degenerate source triangles. Those faces already existed.
- Blender 5.3.0 live MCP authoring and six matched Cycles previews completed.
  The original `orbital_visitors.blend` SHA-256 remains
  `29719efc747cffbb15f055e3645aac36c05ad70b46477c3e481cbf5fdf9a3399`.
- Runtime/canonical/staged ISS GLB SHA-256:
  `065821bc18be607064d58fa6cfc34db059d79da6f4a673d55e2fba1afe0a2c6b`.

## Unreal interoperability

Unreal 5.8.3 imported and saved one mesh, four materials and eight textures in
the existing asset sandbox. A separate process reload checks the same source
hash. Nanite is disabled; there is one LOD.

The sandbox render mesh contains 35,526 triangles. An exact multiset comparison
of all four material sections, after the standard axis and meter-to-centimeter
conversion, proves every nonzero-area source triangle is retained. Unreal omits
2,339 exactly zero-area source triangles (1,795 thermal and 544 foil); it does
not omit visible geometry. The Quest GLB retains all 37,865 triangles. The
Khronos index-degeneracy count alone does not count collinear triangles.

The final import and reload run with NullRHI. This is content/import proof, not
an Unreal image or Quest rendering benchmark. An earlier diagnostic process
crashed during shutdown after writing its results; the explicit duplicate quit
call was removed before final validation.

## Unlocked Quest smoke test — 2026-11-12

The unlocked Quest launched the broader development APK successfully. Its
installed SHA-256 matched
`73637f6253b07f9092774fbeaabc7943cf77219ccacf1a9732b8d4927000f8c4`.
The APK contains the same reviewed ISS GLB as this branch. An actual stereo
Quest display capture (`art/validation/quest-20261112/normal-display.png` in
the development checkout) shows the ISS flyby above Earth, together with the
space background and other scene assets. This is distant flyby visibility,
not close material or interaction acceptance. Raw captures remain local.

Runtime logs recorded the ISS becoming visible and showed no fatal-exception
markers in the captured windows. After the wearer confirmed the app was
visible, a later current-build 20-sample window reported 90–91 FPS, median
App time 7.76 ms and zero stale frames. Other startup and imagery-loading
windows had stalls. These measurements include unrelated development changes
and do not isolate the ISS upgrade's cost or prove a performance improvement.
A full-sphere/partitioned Earth comparison was also inconclusive because
visibility, temperature and timing conditions differed.

The updated development APK was restored after testing and launched without
diagnostic extras. The isolated ISS PR remains a draft pending close material,
interaction and controlled performance acceptance; no merge is implied.
