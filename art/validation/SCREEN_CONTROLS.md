## Gestures and frame

- Hold one open hand up in front of you, palm away from your face, for 500 ms to
  pause. A second hand resting below the raised-hand area does not block it.
  Lower the stop hand before repeating. Pause is explicit and cannot toggle
  paused media back into playback, including buffering and pending-surface cases.
- Raise both open hands, palms away, and push together to move the screen back.
  Turn both palms toward your face and pull together to bring it closer. Both
  hands must travel at least 5.5 cm to engage. Each metre of hand travel maps to
  four metres of screen movement, bounded between 1.5 and 6 metres from the viewer.
  This changes distance without changing screen size. Pulling also restores a
  hidden screen into the current view, even after turning away from its old position.
- The existing inward clap hides the screen; spreading facing palms restores it.
  Two pinches still resize. Pinches, controller grips, navigation, native frame
  manipulation, and visitor/Earth grabs suppress conflicting open-hand gestures.
- Tracking loss and implausible hand jumps cancel depth movement. A single moving
  hand or lateral stroke cannot engage two-hand depth movement.

Playback previously hid the library entity and explicitly disabled its native
resize component. That entity owns the ISDK frame. It now stays visible with a
transparent interior while playing, and native grabbing/resizing stays enabled
until the screen is hidden. The handle depth offset is in front of the video.
SDK manipulation owns input so a corner drag cannot also become a screen tap or
two-hand resize. Video, subtitles, and side controls follow the same screen transform.

The physical side menu grows from 0.56 × 1.64 m to 0.84 × 2.0 m. Primary labels use
26–38 sp and brighter secondary text. Its 560 × 1640 display remains unchanged.
The library retains its 1600 × 900 display with matching 160 dpi layout coordinates.
Scene assets, textures, geometry, shaders, posters, and video quality are preserved.
