# Cabin validation — November 14, 2026

The cabin was authored through the live Blender MCP session in Blender 5.3 and
integrated into the native app on `codex/cabin-realism`, based on `2a0d605`.
The changes are local; no release, push or headset installation was performed.
The user requested finishing the assets and build, deferring the headset check.

The added furniture, structural details and surface finishes increase the asset
cost. All original shell triangles are retained exactly. No source geometry was
decimated and no textures were reduced in resolution. The export uses one
primitive per mesh, with fewer than 60,000 vertices each and 16-bit indices.

approximately 26.67 MiB; this is an estimate, not a measured Quest allocation.

The geometry audit verifies finite positions, unit normals, orthogonal unit
tangents, valid indices, identity node transforms, core glTF materials, embedded
1024² textures, and clear seat/default-screen volumes. The shell bounds are
unchanged. The Khronos validator reports zero errors, warnings, infos and hints.

## Visual and authoring checks

The exported GLB was reimported into Blender and rendered at 1920 × 1234. The
overview and bench/console close-up were inspected. Faceted cushion corners found
during review were corrected with smooth bevel normals before the final export.
The editable `.blend` contains the authoring scene, original reference and final
GLB review, with packed texture data and relative source paths.

Unreal imported 11 meshes, 11 materials and five 1024² textures, preserving all
115,544 triangles. The saved review map is
`/Game/MetaTheatre/Cabin20261114/ObservationCabin`. Its Vulkan viewport capture is
separate from the Blender preview and from native headset evidence.
The final Vulkan capture completed and the editor exited successfully.
