# Roadster Quest surface repair — November 14, 2026

The wearer confirmed **body and lighting look good** on the final installed
build. Actual Quest captures show a continuous body, replacing the red/black

## Repair and evidence

The original GLB put ten material primitives in one mesh. Some primitives use
POSITION/NORMAL; others also use UVs and tangents. Separating those primitives
into ten meshes, under identity child nodes of the original root, fixes the
visible defect in this SDK/device combination. This establishes a working
import layout; the SDK's internal cause has not been proven.

`scripts/separate_roadster_meshes.py` performs the conversion. The finish export
script now calls it. The original root retains its transform and scene
references. Primitive definitions, accessor definitions, buffer views and
materials are unchanged. Images, indices and the complete binary payload are
byte-for-byte unchanged. The editable source remains
`blender/space_roadster_finish_20261113.blend`; no geometry authoring change was
needed. Live Blender inspection and exported-geometry checks found no broad
duplicate-body or reversed-normal defect in the sampled body surfaces.

The conversion preserves **136,900 triangles, ten material primitives and five
512-square images**. It adds ten hierarchy nodes and 780 bytes of JSON, bringing
the GLB to **4,544,476 bytes**. There is no extra material draw, texture upload
or simplified geometry. Node overhead and sustained frame time were not
benchmarked, so no FPS improvement is claimed.

The fixed asset is identical in `exports/starship_20261113/`,
`exports/flybys/` and `app/src/main/assets/`. SHA-256:

```

```

## Lighting and presentation

After confirming the solid mesh, the wearer reported that lighting looked off.
The flyby previously presented the dark undertray toward the seat. Its pitch
changes from -25 to +25 degrees, exposing the hood and cockpit throughout the
pass. Position, scale, timing and slow roll are retained.

Shared SDK ambient fill changes from `(0.06, 0.07, 0.09)` to
`(0.22, 0.25, 0.30)` to keep shaded paint and trim readable. Direct sunlight,
Sun direction, environment intensity and all materials remain unchanged.
This also affects the other SDK-lit vehicles and chair. Custom Earth/planet
shaders are unchanged, and Quiet cinema retains its existing dimming factors.
No extra light or rendering pass is added. The wearer then confirmed both the
body and lighting look good.
